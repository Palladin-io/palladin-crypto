# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Palladin contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to providing a
welcoming, safe, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual technical or security impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully and keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.
- Put user safety and Palladin's zero-knowledge security model first.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
publication of another person's private information, or public disclosure of an
unresolved vulnerability is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in Palladin repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and other project spaces, including when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **patryk.roguszewski@palladin.io** with
`Code of Conduct` in the subject. Reports will be handled confidentially and
reviewed for conflicts of interest.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
or permanently exclude a participant when necessary. Retaliation against a
reporter or witness is prohibited.

Security vulnerabilities are handled under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not
through public conduct reports.
